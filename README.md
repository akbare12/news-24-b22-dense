# Tuesday 24 November 2026 in one picture

A dense front page of 38 verified stories from Tuesday 24 November 2026: the Houthi drone strike on Riyadh's PIF tower, the probe into Shanghai party chief Chen Jining, and Huawei's guilty verdict on 10 of 12 counts lead 13 illustrated panels, a world map with 13 numbered pins, and a date check separating 24 Nov news from what was left out or is not yet.

Built with Python and Pillow only: `python3 main.py` renders a deterministic 1024×1024 `entry.png` to `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `out/`).
